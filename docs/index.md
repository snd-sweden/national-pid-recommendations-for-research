# National PID recommendations for research

The *National PID recommendations for research* is a Swedish resource for PID usage in the research sector.

It has been created to provide an overview of the PID infrastructures currently available and how they may be used by research organisations in a responsible manner, enabling a friendly, well described and interconnected open research landscape.

1. [Introduction](./introduction/why.md) – why PID recommendations are needed in Sweden, and the gaps they can help close.
2. [PID concepts](./pid-concepts/index.md) – gives an overview of some PID-specific terminology and definitions.
3. [Landscape analysis](./landscape-analysis/index.md) – explores the current landscape of PID infrastructures for objects and phenomena relating to research.
4. [PID strategies in other countries](./international/index.md) – national PID strategies, roadmaps and analyses published in other countries and by international organisations, and the themes they share.
5. [Recommendations](./recommendations/index.md) – suggested PID solutions for Swedish research organisations to use and implement.
6. [PID actors in Sweden](./pid-actors-sweden/index.md) – organisations in Sweden providing or coordinating PID services.
7. [Data on PIDs](./data-on-pids/index.md) – formal descriptions of various PID systems, including facts, references and how to get started using them.

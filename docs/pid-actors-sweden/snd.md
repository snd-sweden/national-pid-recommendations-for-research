# Swedish National Data Service (SND)

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

The Swedish National Data Service (SND) is a national research infrastructure supporting the accessibility, preservation and reuse of research data. SND is funded by the Swedish Research Council together with a consortium of Swedish higher education institutions, and is hosted by the University of Gothenburg. SND provides PID services for Swedish research organisations and research infrastructures, and leads the Swedish DataCite consortium.

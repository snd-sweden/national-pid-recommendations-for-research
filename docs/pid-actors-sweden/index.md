# PID actors in Sweden

This chapter contains information on organisations in Sweden that provide or coordinate PID services for the research sector. Most PID systems are governed internationally, and Swedish organisations typically get access to them through a national consortium or a national registration agency. The organisations described here fill such roles in the [PID ecosystem](../pid-concepts/pid-ecosystem.md), for example as consortium leads, PID providers or national namespace providers:

- [Sunet](sunet.md) - leads the Swedish ORCID consortium.
- [National Library of Sweden (KB)](kb.md) - provides URN:NBN, ISBN and ISSN for Swedish publications and maintains the Libris authority file.
- [Swedish National Data Service (SND)](snd.md) - leads the Swedish DataCite consortium and provides ePIC PIDs.

Each page lists the PID services and roles of the organisation, with links to where to get started.

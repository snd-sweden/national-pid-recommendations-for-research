# Sunet

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

Sunet (Swedish University Computer Network) provides network infrastructure and related services to Swedish higher education institutions and other research organisations. Sunet is part of the Swedish Research Council (Vetenskapsrådet). Besides network connectivity, Sunet provides services for identity and access management, including the Swedish ORCID consortium and the SWAMID identity federation.

## PID services and roles

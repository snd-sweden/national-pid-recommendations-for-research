# National Library of Sweden (KB)

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

The National Library of Sweden (Kungliga biblioteket, KB) is a government agency responsible for collecting, preserving and providing access to material published in Sweden. KB maintains the national library catalogue Libris and the national publication index [SwePub](https://swepub.kb.se), and acts as the Swedish registration agency for several bibliographic identifiers.

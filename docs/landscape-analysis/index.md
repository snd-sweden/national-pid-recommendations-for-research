# Landscape analysis of PIDs in research

This landscape analysis chapter explores the current landscape of PIDs and PID infrastructures for objects and phenomena relating to research. These include people and organisations, research outputs such as publications, data and software, and entities such as projects, grants, instruments and research infrastructures.

Each page describes what is identified in that area and why identification is needed. It then covers the PIDs and other identifiers in use, their strengths and limitations, and how they relate to each other. The analysis provides the background for the [scope-specific recommendations](../recommendations/scope-specific/index.md), and detailed facts about each PID system are given in [Data on PIDs](../data-on-pids/index.md).

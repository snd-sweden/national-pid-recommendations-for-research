# Recommendations

This chapter contains recommendations for responsible PID usage in Swedish research. It builds on the [PID concepts](../pid-concepts/index.md) and the [landscape analysis](../landscape-analysis/index.md), and is divided into three parts:

- [Generic recommendations for PID usage](generic/index.md) - recommendations that apply regardless of PID system or scope: how to reference, choose, assign and maintain PIDs, and how organisations should organise their PID work.
- [National coordination of PID usage](national/index.md) - recommendations for national actors and research organisations on coordinating PID usage across the research sector.
- [Scope-specific recommendations](scope-specific/index.md) - recommendations that point out specific PID systems and providers for identifying specific digital objects and phenomena.

Each recommendation starts with a short statement, followed by **Why?**, explaining the reasons behind it, and **How?**, giving practical guidance on implementation.

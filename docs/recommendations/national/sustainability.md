# Sustainability and competence

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

These recommendations concern the awareness and competence needed to use PIDs across the sector, the recognition of outputs identified with PIDs in research assessment, and the long-term sustainability of the PID infrastructures that Swedish research depends on.

## Build awareness and competence across the sector

_Applies to: national actors and research organisations._

!!! quote ""

    Develop shared guidance, training and communication on PIDs, adapted to the needs of different target groups.

??? question "Why?"

    Information about PIDs is scattered, and PIDs are often seen as an administrative burden rather than a benefit. Researchers rarely choose identifiers themselves, but research support staff and system owners need to know which PIDs to use and recommend, and how. Shared material and training avoid duplicated effort across organisations and give a consistent message.

??? info "How?"

    - Maintain shared, up-to-date guidance, such as these recommendations and [Data on PIDs](../../data-on-pids/index.md), organised by PID system and by the reader's role, with named owners for each part.
    - Offer training for research support staff, system owners and researchers, based on identified competence needs and concrete examples.
    - Combine national communication with activities within organisations and research communities, and adapt the message to the needs of different disciplines.
    - Show the benefits of PIDs with concrete use cases, such as reduced reporting effort and better visibility of research.

See also: [PID concepts](../../pid-concepts/index.md)

## Recognise outputs identified with PIDs in research assessment

_Applies to: research funders and research organisations._

!!! quote ""

    Take data, software and other outputs identified with PIDs into account in research assessment, recruitment, promotion and funding decisions, in line with responsible research assessment. Do not only consider published results.

??? question "Why?"

    PIDs make research outputs beyond publications identifiable, citable and traceable, but researchers have little reason to invest in well-described, traceable outputs if these are not recognised. Initiatives for responsible research assessment, such as the Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment (CoARA), call for recognising a broader range of outputs and practices, and PIDs make such recognition practical.

??? info "How?"

    - Let applicants and candidates list data, software and other outputs with their PIDs, for example by importing them from their ORCID records.
    - Assess the quality of such outputs, including how well they are described, linked and made reusable, rather than only counting them.
    - As a research organisation, follow up the data publications and other outputs of affiliated researchers through their PIDs, and highlight good examples.
    - Work towards a shared national understanding of how such outputs are recognised in assessment.

See also: [Research projects and research activities](../../landscape-analysis/projects-activities.md) · [Research data](../../landscape-analysis/research-data.md) · [Research software and models](../../landscape-analysis/software-models.md)

## Assess national dependencies and secure sustainable funding

_Applies to: national actors._

!!! quote ""

    Assess the national dependence on PID infrastructures, and secure lasting funding for the national services and consortia that Swedish PID usage relies on.

??? question "Why?"

    PIDs are only as persistent as the infrastructures and organisations behind them. National consortia, resolvers and registration services are shared infrastructure, and short-term funding puts the PIDs they manage at risk. Some risks, such as changes in the ownership or conditions of an international PID provider, or legal questions about where metadata is stored and processed, concern the whole sector and are best assessed jointly.

??? info "How?"

    - Carry out a national risk analysis of the PID infrastructures that Swedish research depends on, covering organisational, economic, technical and legal risks.
    - Identify the national services that are critical for registering and resolving PIDs, and make sure that they have long-term funding and continuity plans.
    - Consider national measures for critical PIDs, such as copies of registration metadata held in Sweden or agreements on transferring responsibility if a service is discontinued.

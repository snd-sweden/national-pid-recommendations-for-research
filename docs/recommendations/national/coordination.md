# Organising national coordination

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

These recommendations concern how organisations take part in national coordination of PID usage, and how Swedish participation in international PID work is coordinated.

## Take part in national coordination of PID usage

_Applies to: national actors and research organisations._

!!! quote ""

    Take part in, or stay informed about, national coordination of PID usage in Sweden, the national consortia for the PID systems you use, and relevant international initiatives.

??? question "Why?"

    The value of each organisation's PID work depends on how other organisations identify and link the same people, organisations, grants and outputs. Many challenges, such as gaps in the PID landscape, choices between competing PID systems or the requirements to put on system vendors, are too large for a single organisation to solve, while shared solutions reduce the cost for everyone.

??? info "How?"

    - Join or follow the national consortia for the PID systems you use, such as the Swedish DataCite consortium led by [SND](../../pid-actors-sweden/snd.md) or the Swedish ORCID consortium maintained by [Sunet](../../pid-actors-sweden/sunet.md).
    - Share experiences and implementation practices with other organisations.
    - Raise gaps in the Swedish PID landscape where they can be addressed jointly.

See also: [PID actors in Sweden](../../pid-actors-sweden/index.md)

## Coordinate Swedish participation in international PID work

_Applies to: national actors._

!!! quote ""

    Coordinate Swedish participation in the governance of international PID infrastructures and in international work on PID policies and standards.

??? question "Why?"

    Most PID systems used in Swedish research are governed internationally, and decisions about their development, costs and conditions are made outside Sweden. Coordinated participation gives Swedish research a stronger voice in these decisions, makes it possible to learn from other countries' national PID strategies, and helps keep Swedish practices aligned with European and international frameworks.

??? info "How?"

    - Represent Swedish interests in the governance of the PID infrastructures that Swedish research depends on, directly or through national consortia.
    - Follow and contribute to international coordination of national PID strategies, and reuse solutions from other countries where they fit.
    - Keep national recommendations aligned with European PID policies, and report international developments back to Swedish organisations.

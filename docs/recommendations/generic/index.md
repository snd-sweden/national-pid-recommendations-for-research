# Generic recommendations for PID usage

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

## Purpose and scope

This section contains recommendations that apply regardless of which PID system is used or what is identified. They describe how to reference, choose, assign and maintain PIDs in a responsible manner, and how organisations should organise their PID work. Recommendations for specific scopes, such as researchers, organisations, publications, research data, software or grants, and for specific PID systems and providers, are given in the [scope-specific recommendations](../scope-specific/index.md). Recommendations on coordinating PID usage at the national level are given in [National coordination of PID usage](../national/index.md).

Each recommendation states who it applies to, using the roles described in the [PID ecosystem](../../pid-concepts/pid-ecosystem.md):

- **All PID Users** - anyone who uses PIDs in references and metadata, including researchers, repositories, publishers, funders and research information systems.
- **PID Managers** - organisations that register PIDs and maintain their targets and metadata, for example through a repository or research infrastructure.
- **Organisations selecting PID services** - organisations deciding which PID systems and providers to adopt for their own services.

Many organisations act in all three roles at once.

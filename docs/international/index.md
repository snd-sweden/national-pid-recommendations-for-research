Several countries have published national strategies, roadmaps or recommendations for the use of persistent identifiers (PIDs) in research. They differ in scope and in who has developed them, but they address many of the same questions: which PID systems to prioritise, how to integrate PIDs into research systems and workflows, how to fund and govern the national PID infrastructure, and how to build awareness and competence. Some have published preparatory outputs. International organisations have published reviews, recommendations and checklists on how to best proceed on a national level.

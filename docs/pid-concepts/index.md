# PID concepts

This chapter explains concepts and terms relating to PID systems and PID usage. It examines what PIDs are and continues with the properties that make an identifier reliable over time, such as persistence, uniqueness, resolvability and interpretability. It then covers practical parts of PID management, such as kernel metadata, landing pages and tombstone pages.

The section ends with the [PID ecosystem](pid-ecosystem.md), which describes the roles of the actors that together provide and use PIDs. These concepts and roles are used throughout the landscape analysis and the recommendations.
